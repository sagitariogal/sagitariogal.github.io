  Commission includes a custom coloured illustration made in my style, with one colour background. Can include from 1 to 2 characters, with simple decorations around, for example a frame, different items etc. More complex background is possible to add (fee applied as an add-on).
  
  Default (included colored background): 30€
  + Character: 5€ per
  + Complex background/decorations: 10€
  + Commercial use fee: 15€
  
  Get a quote/request a commission: <href -> ko-fi>
  
  ## doodles
  Commission includes a quick doodle illustration made in my style, with one colour background. Can include from 1 to 2 characters and simple accessories, elements of nature (flowers, branches etc.). 
  
  Default package: 5€
  + character: 2€ per
  + Commercial use fee: 2.5€
  
  ## lineart
  Commission includes a custom lineart illustration made in my style, with one colour background. Can include from 1 to 2 characters, with simple decorations around, for example a frame, different items etc.
